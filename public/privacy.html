Privacy Policy — אותיות ומספרים לגיל 4

Package ID: com.learningapp.hebrewkids • Updated: November 2026

1. Designed for Children & Families

This app is built for 4-year-old children learning Hebrew letters, vowels, and numbers. We show zero advertisements, use zero third-party analytics or ad trackers, and never require children to create an account.

2. Local & Optional Cloud Progress

All voice audio and fonts are bundled offline. By default, star rewards, gender voice preference, and sticker board progress are stored solely in local device storage.

Parents may optionally pass the arithmetic Parental Gate and sign in with Google in the Parent Zone to back up their child’s learning progress (stars, daily streak, unlocked Pro access) to our Cloudflare D1 database. Only basic Google profile identifiers (user ID, display name, email, avatar URL) are stored for that parent session, and you may sign out at any time.

3. Parental Gate & Purchases

All settings, language switches, external links, and Pro upgrade options are protected behind an arithmetic Parental Gate in compliance with Google Play’s Designed for Families policy.
